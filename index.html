Our story

The smell of fresh-baked bread reminds us of what is most important: stillness, comfort, and togetherness.

Now imagine that on an ordinary Tuesday. A warm loaf on the dinner table. A sandwich cut fresh for a lunchbox. A few quiet minutes together at the end of a tired day.

Let us take a “loaf” off your day by adding one to your plate.

Why it’s hard

TimeProofing, planning, so many steps. Homemade bread mostly happens on weekends.
CostGood bread is expensive, and delivery fees pile on top.
FreshnessStale loaves and preservatives. Ours has four ingredients and nothing else.

What our survey found

The biggest hurdle to fresh bread

Time and effortProofing, many steps, and advance planning keep homemade baking to weekends.
AccessFew local bakers or stores, and limited options in mainstream grocery stores.
CostFresh bread is expensive.
Know-howNot knowing how to bake, and having no one to bake for you.

The biggest problems with delivery

CostHigh delivery fees, taxes, and shipping prices.
FreshnessBread arrives stale, goes moldy fast, or is loaded with preservatives.
AvailabilityServices are missing in some areas or lack options.
Speed and careSlow delivery, and items handled carelessly.

Data found from our 2026 survey. We asked, and you answered.
